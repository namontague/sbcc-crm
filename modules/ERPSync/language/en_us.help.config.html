ERPSync

Use this page to configure settings to connect to an ERP

In the ERPSync Options sub-panel, enter information for the following fields:

  • URL. Enter the web address of the ERP Gateway.
  • Username. Enter the a username that has access to the gateway you need.
  • Password Enter the password for the user.

To save your ERPSync Gateway settings, click Save; click Cancel to exit the page without saving your changes.
